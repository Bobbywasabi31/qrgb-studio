How this works

Every data module represents one of five symbols (base 5). Each group of 14 modules stores 4 bytes. The three color-coded finder markers define orientation. Version 2 encodes data with Reed–Solomon RS(255,239), correcting up to eight arbitrary erroneous bytes per codeword, then verifies the CRC-32. Photos use a four-point projective transform selected by the user.

This is a new QRGB format, not a standard QR code. Ordinary phone QR readers cannot scan it. v2 adds byte-level error correction, sampled color calibration, and manually guided perspective correction. It cannot guarantee reliable results from low-resolution photos; print and camera testing are still needed. Original v1 PNGs remain readable.
