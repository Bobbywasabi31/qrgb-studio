How this works

Every data module represents one of five symbols (base 5). Each group of 14 modules stores 4 bytes; 5¹⁴ is larger than 2³². Three finder markers identify the corners. A small header stores the content length and a CRC-32 checksum. The reader verifies the checksum before accepting the recovered file.

This is a new QRGB format, not a standard QR code. Ordinary phone QR readers cannot scan it. It has no forward error correction yet. Use original lossless PNGs to preserve all colors; scanning from print or camera will require calibration, perspective correction, and error correction.
